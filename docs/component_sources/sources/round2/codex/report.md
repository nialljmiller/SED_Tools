## Independent evidence findings

**2MASS — correction confirmed.** [System transmission](https://www.ipac.caltech.edu/2mass/releases/allsky/doc/sec3_1b1.html) publishes separate mirror, window, lens coatings, dichroics, filters, NICMOS3 QE and atmosphere, plus products with/without atmosphere. Representative mirror, J-filter, QE, atmosphere and combined-table bodies were fetched, and page definitions examined. [Absolute calibration](https://www.ipac.caltech.edu/2mass/releases/allsky/doc/sec6_4a.html) explicitly explains wavelength-weighted, peak-normalized RSRs for direct integration with F_lambda, PLEXUS atmosphere and approximately 5 mm water for the J calibration. Raw optics*QE*filter is a different quantity. Do not multiply the published RSR by wavelength again. QE is characteristic, not measured for each installed detector. Mirror data are per reflection, with two reflections before the camera. Grok's historical correction is sound; total-only is false. Exact reconstruction of every band was not performed.

**LSST — components real, mapping unresolved.** Opened upstream filter_g, m1, detector, hardware_g, total_g, atmos_std and atmos_10. Numeric headers say nm, 0–1 and Version 1.9. [Baseline README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lsst/throughputs/main/baseline/README.md) distinguishes hardware/total; its 1.2-airmass and zenith descriptions conflict. SVO LSST.g independently says 1.5. Builder tag v1.9.2 writing LSST/LSST is an applicability mismatch, not proof that SVO v1.5 is reproduced. Atmosphere headers do not establish PWV or the meaning of 10. No exact v1.5/v1.9 product reconstruction or counting-convention validation completed.

**HSC — filter generations must remain separate.** [Old r](https://subarutelescope.org/Instruments/HSC/txt/wHSC-r.txt) has 126 rows, [r2](https://subarutelescope.org/Instruments/HSC/txt/wHSC-r2.txt) 582, with angstrom headers and clearly different samples. Old r includes a small negative measured value. Representative g/QE/PFU/M1/atmosphere bodies were fetched; only r/r2 parsed for this comparison. The fixed commit README URL ending `/README` returned 404; do not cite that URL as successfully retrieved model documentation. Historical claimed X=1.2/PWV=1.5 remains a researcher claim pending a working definition source. Old r/i can be appropriate for an old key; do not silently replace them with r2/i2. i/i2 applicability remains unverified here.

**VISTA — mixed wavelength as well as response scales.** The [ESO bundle](https://www.eso.org/sci/facilities/paranal/decommissioned/vircam/inst/Filters_QE_Atm_curves.tar.gz) is a real 19-member tar. Inspected qe.tab, both trans files and f02.sm after gzip decompression. Hardware uses nm/percent as supported by the plotting script; atmosphere x values are already micrometres and y fractional. Headers give 1 mm/X=1 and 3 mm/X=2. Atmosphere model/version not established. Builder uses SkyCalc instead; that is a derived substitution. Current hardware helper's percent scale is appropriate for hardware; it must not be applied to supplied atmosphere. Edge-held mirror tails are a builder assumption. All narrow-band temperature claims remain unverified.

**ZTF — mixture is derived.** Pinned vendor g CSV parses to 2593 rows, peak 93.7918098; single/multi QE parse to 230/180 rows, maxima 0.9151245552/0.9256740184. Files lack explicit unit headers. [Repository README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZwickyTransientFacility/ztf_information/7c1d1ed1adeea47ee024272e797e77d7e65160d5/README.md) establishes coating populations; SVO g explicitly says multi-layer coating. Equal-weight mean is an approximate camera product, not that published curve. No Palomar atmosphere/complete optics proof was established. Repository LICENSE was fetched, but source-specific redistribution scope was not resolved.

**JWST — archive mismatch confirmed numerically.** [Individual F200W](https://jwst-docs.stsci.edu/files/216457506/216457520/1/1762454205059/F200W_mean_system_throughput.txt) has micrometre/throughput columns. The [v5 zip](https://jwst-docs.stsci.edu/files/216457506/216457550/1/1762454237820/nircam_throughputs_4Nov2022_v5.zip) has 209 entries; its mean F200W differs at 1.6874000244 micrometres: 8.3369257969e-5 versus 8.0170569720e-5. This verifies difference, not every band's release mapping. Do not use the zip as a v7/2025 product. Original NIRCam and NIRCam2025 keys still require release-specific attribution. Pandeia Box retrieval did not complete and was interrupted; no archive contents, component paths or version applicability were independently verified. No multi-GB bundle was saved.

**Euclid — separability confirmed for NISP Y.** [ESA definition](https://euclid.esac.esa.int/msp/refdata/nisp/NISP-PHOTO-PASSBANDS-V1) defines ground/prelaunch focal-plane-centre passbands; telescope includes mirrors and dichroic. [Y file](https://euclid.esac.esa.int/msp/refdata/data/NISP-PHOTO-PASSBANDS-V1-Y_throughput.dat) parses to 341 rows/six columns. The four factors reproduce T_TOTAL within maximum absolute residual 4.981e-7, consistent with tabulation precision. No units appear in its header; nm remains plausible, not proved by this header. Keep telescope dichroic separate from NI-OA conceptually; do not double multiply it. J/H, FITS units, newer releases and SVO exact mapping remain unverified. VIS historical limited search does not demonstrate nonexistence and receives no independent absence certification.

**Cassini — pair versus individual filter.** [PDS efficiency definitions](https://pds-rings.seti.org/holdings/volumes/COISS_0xxx/COISS_0011/calib/efficiency/efficiency_info.txt), ccdqe.tab, na_optics.tab, wa_optics.tab and representative issnabl1cl2_systrans.tab were inspected. CCD header gives electrons/photon, nm and separate NAC/WAC columns; optics are separate. Systrans column 2 is QE*optics*filter1*filter2; column 3 additionally contains solar flux. Division by QE*optics recovers the pair, including CL transmission. A clear filter is not demonstrated to be identically one. Restrict division to valid overlapping support and calibration version; polarizer orientation matters. No actual recovered individual-filter curve or complete sibling-file validation was performed.

**IMX455 — provenance not established.** argus.py reads a local digitized CSV and sets metadata; it does not supply the originating datasheet. The [cited Sony page](https://www.sony-semicon.com/en/products/is/industry/gs-bsi.html) contains no IMX455 reference. Treat this as a builder lead, not original sensor verification. Monochrome versus colour variant, absolute QE normalization, digitization and licence all need primary evidence.

**Generic systems — convention evidence is narrower than claimed.** Independently parsed SVO Generic/Bessell.U and Generic/Johnson.U give DetectorType=0 and components=Filter. That confirms aggregator metadata, not universal physical decomposition. [Bessell 1990 original paper](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1990PASP..102.1181B) was fetched and text inspected: it constructs representative synthetic standard passbands and discusses detector/filter combinations. It does not supply one universal telescope/QE decomposition for these generic systems. Exact original table-to-SVO numerical matching and integration convention were not completed. Other generic keys, JHKLM atmosphere conditions, Johnson original tables and all sibling bands remain unverified. Preserve energy/photon metadata as source-specific pending convention audit; avoid imposing another detector response on an abstract standard bandpass.
